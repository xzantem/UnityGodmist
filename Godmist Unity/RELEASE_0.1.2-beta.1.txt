Godmist 0.1.2 Beta 1 — Early Access

A preview of the upcoming 0.1.2 update, available for testing and feedback.
This is a beta prerelease, not the final 0.1.2 release.

WHAT'S NEW
- Initial controller support, including movement and camera control, skill and
  target selection, party and panel menus, inventory context actions and an
  on-screen keyboard.
- A macOS Universal build for Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

FIXES AND IMPROVEMENTS
- Enemies move continuously during combat instead of pausing at each tile.
- Hover outlines are included correctly in standalone builds.
- Inventory stacks keep a consistent order when items are used, sold or moved:
  item type, rarity, name, then price.
- Improved catacomb wall junctions, corner faces, end caps and masking.
- Moving with a controller works with an unlocked camera, without requiring
  a recenter first. Moving no longer leaves the leader's tooltip on screen.
- Improved opening and closing of the combat item picker.

CONTROLLER QUICK GUIDE
Left stick: movement / selection. Right stick: camera / scrolling.
A: confirm or interact. Hold A while exploring: nearby interactions.
B: back or cancel. X: context actions. Y: details / confirm multiple targets.
Hold Y during your turn, outside targeting and menus: end turn.
LT: party. RT: panels, including combat items. LB/RB: skills or skill pages;
while targeting, rotate supported areas.
D-pad left/right: targets. D-pad up/down: zoom.
L3: toggle free cursor (left stick). R3: recenter camera.
Back/View: map. Start/Menu: pause.

TESTING NOTES
Controller support is experimental. Please report awkward controls or blocked
interactions. The macOS build still needs testing on actual Mac hardware and
is not notarized by Apple. See the included launch and log-collection guide.
Existing saves retain their location and compatibility.
