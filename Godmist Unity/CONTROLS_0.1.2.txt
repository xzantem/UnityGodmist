KEYBOARD AND MOUSE
I: Party View. Select each companion's Equipment, Statistics, Skills or
Development tab independently. Use the header to open the individual card.
Items: left-click selects; double-click equips or uses; right-click opens actions.
Drag an item onto another companion's portrait or column to transfer it.
Skill targeting: choose a target and confirm; an out-of-range target shows the
approach before your character moves and casts. Outside combat, movement
remains available while aiming.
Camera: F changes camera follow. Middle/right drag pans an unlocked exploration
camera; right drag also unlocks it. Scroll zooms.
Follow the on-screen contextual hints for combat actions and other shortcuts.

XBOX-STYLE CONTROLLER
Left stick: exploration movement / combat tile / menu selection.
Right stick: camera / menu scrolling. R3: recenter camera.
A: interact or confirm. Hold A in exploration: nearby interactions.
B: back, cancel or return from free cursor.
X: context actions; in Party View, additional windows when not focusing an item.
Y: inspection; while choosing multiple skill targets, confirm that selection.
Hold Y during your turn outside targeting and menus: end turn.
LT: Party View. LB/RB in Party View: select a companion column.
RT: action wheel. While aiming, LB/RB rotate supported areas.
LB/RB outside combat and targeting: companion selection.
D-pad left/right: cycle targets. D-pad up/down: zoom.
L3: toggle precise cursor. Back/View: map. Start/Menu: pause.
Some controls depend on context; the on-screen hints show the current action.
Controller availability depends on the device and operating-system drivers.
