Godmist 0.1.2 — Early Access

PARTY AND INVENTORY
- New Party View brings every companion's equipment, backpack, statistics,
  skills and development into one screen. Each column can show a different tab.
- Transfer items directly between companions, including by dropping an item
  onto a portrait. Stacks offer a quantity selector and a carrying-capacity preview.
- Click to select, double-click to equip or use, and right-click for actions.
  Hover tooltips replace the redundant item-details panel.
- Blocked item actions explain why they cannot be used.
- Inventory stacks keep their order when their quantity changes.

COMBAT AND EXPLORATION
- Choosing an out-of-range skill target now previews the approach, moves your
  character into range and uses the skill. This also works when opening combat.
- Approaches use the lowest available movement cost and follow moving targets.
- You can keep moving while aiming a skill outside combat.
- Revised highlights make movement, targets, areas and interactions easier to read.
- Unavailable skill icons are dimmed and desaturated; frames and shortcuts stay clear.
- New combat action icons for ending a turn, fleeing and choosing an item.
- Enemies move continuously in combat instead of pausing at every tile.
- Improved catacomb wall joins, corner faces, caps and floor masking.
- Hover outlines now appear correctly in standalone builds.
- Pan an unlocked exploration camera with the middle or right mouse button.
  Dragging with the right button also unlocks a following camera.

CONTROLLER AND PLATFORMS
- Controller support for exploration, combat targeting, action wheels,
  Party View, inventory actions and on-screen text entry.
- Improved camera, targeting and panel navigation with contextual button hints.
- macOS Universal download for Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

Open CONTROLS.txt for a quick guide and READ-ME.txt for launch instructions.
Existing saves retain their location and compatibility.
The macOS build still needs testing on actual Mac hardware and is not notarized.
